# Co-Create — Design Partner Guide

For the first 5–15 writers. One focused session; honest reactions; nothing precious.

## Who this is for

Writers whose voice already matters to their audience: newsletter operators, founder-essayists, solo consultants, cultural/strategy writers. If you'd rather not publish than publish in someone else's voice, you're the right person.

## What Co-Create is (one line)

It doesn't write *for* you. It writes *as* you — bring the unfinished self, leave with transformed work that still sounds like you.

## Before your session — bring one thing

- A **messy note**: a voice-note transcript, bullet fragments, a contradiction you can't resolve, a sentence you can't stop thinking about. Do **not** clean it up. Messy is welcome here.
- (Optional, 10 min) 2–3 of your published pieces pasted in, so Co-Create can learn how you sound first. You can also just start and teach it your voice later.

## The session (one sitting, ~30–45 min)

1. **Register** with your invite code.
2. Either **Build your voice** (paste a few posts) or **Just start**.
3. **Bring** — lay down your messy note. Add "the other side" if there is one.
4. **Shape it** — watch the first form arrive, in your cadence.
5. Edit freely. It's yours.
6. Use **one Polish move** (pick whichever pulls you: Tighten, Meet the reader, Open a mirror, Give it want, Make it visible, Clear the static).
7. **Save it** — or reject it. Both are useful data.
8. Answer the three questions when they appear (30 seconds).

## The three questions

1. **Did it sound like you?** (that's the whole product)
2. **How much did you change?** (light / moderate / heavy / rewrite)
3. **Would you use it again next week?**

Plus one free-text line: *what felt off?* Be blunt — flattery teaches us nothing.

## What we promise you

- Your writing and voice stay yours; your data is isolated per account and never used to train anything.
- Concierge support during the pilot: message the founder directly with anything broken or confusing.
- You're shaping the product — the moves, the copy, the defaults will bend toward what you report.

## Founder cadence (internal)

- Publish at least weekly **with** the tool during the pilot — the founder's body of work is the public demo.
- Run `npx tsx scripts/design-partner-readiness.ts` before each new invite wave.
- Review feedback + activation weekly; every "missed me" gets a follow-up conversation.

## Dry-run checklist (run once on production before wave 1)

- [ ] Register a fresh account with the invite code (no founder hand-holding).
- [ ] Reach a first draft in one sitting from a messy note.
- [ ] One Polish move works end-to-end.
- [ ] Save → feedback questions appear → submission lands (check readiness report).
- [ ] Spend cap set for the account (`npx tsx scripts/set-owner-limit.ts`).
